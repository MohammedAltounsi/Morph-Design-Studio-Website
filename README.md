This is the website for Morph, a studio that designs and runs exhibitions and conferences and also does digital marketing and social media management for brands. The site is a single page with a dark background. Each section has its name in Arabic and English.

Designed and built by **Mohammed Altounsi** · [LinkedIn](https://www.linkedin.com/in/mohammed-altounsi/)

## Sections

<img src="assets/readme/sitemap.svg" width="100%" alt="Four numbered sections as in the site navigation: 01 Home, 02 About, 03 Services, 04 Projects, each with its Arabic label">

- Home: 3D glass cubes fill the screen behind a large "LET'S MORPH!" headline.
- About: a short introduction to the studio and its four goals, in Arabic and English.
- Services: graphic design, marketing and events.
- Projects: past work in three groups (conferences and exhibitions, brand identities, advertising).
- Contact: ways to reach the studio. There is also a contact button in the header.

## Arabic and English

- The navigation is in Arabic. An `En` button next to it switches the language.
- Headings show both languages, for example Our Services with خدماتنا and Featured Portfolio with معرض الأعمال.
- On mobile the menu is hidden behind a `+` button and the headline uses the full screen width.
